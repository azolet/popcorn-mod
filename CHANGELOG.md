# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release, for Minecraft 1.21.11 on Fabric.

- **Popcorn** from wheat seeds, in a furnace (10s) or a smoker (5s). A lit furnace or smoker
  with seeds in it pops out loud every couple of seconds.
- **Caramel popcorn**: popcorn with sugar, or three popcorn with a bottle of honey.
- **Popcorn buckets** in red, creeper green and enderman black, folded from five paper and a
  dye. Fill one with eight popcorn — plain or caramel — then eat it, or stand it on a block.
  Up to four fit on one block; right-click a pile with an empty hand to take one back.
